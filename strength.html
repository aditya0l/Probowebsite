Choosing strength equipment.

Three questions our team asks every buyer.

Plate-loaded or selectorised?

Plate-loaded machines feel closer to free weights and suit serious lifters. Selectorised stations with a pin and weight stack are quicker to adjust and easier for beginners. Most commercial floors use both.

How much floor space does a strength zone need?

Allow the machine footprint plus at least 60 cm clear on every side. Use Will it fit on any product page, or the planner for a whole zone.

Can I try machines before buying?

Yes. Every machine on the site is on the showroom floor in Jaipur. Book a visit and the team will set up the ones you want to try.
