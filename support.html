Common questions.

Where is the serial number?

On the metal plate fitted to the frame of your machine, like the example above.

How do I book a service?

Choose Service above, describe the problem and send it on WhatsApp. The desk for your state replies with a visit date.

What does the warranty cover?

Warranty terms depend on the machine. The full terms will be published here.

Can I order a part myself?

Yes. Choose Spares above with your serial number and the part you need.
