ShriRajendra Motwani Ji

Founder, Pro Bodyline Fitness

He founded Pro Bodyline Fitness in 1999 with one aim: to give Indian gyms equipment as good as any in the world.

The brand grew out of his passion for health, innovation and good design. Under his leadership it became a name gym owners trust for quality, durability and performance.

His standard still runs the company: keep innovating, keep pushing, and stay committed to every gym we fit out.

Quality, service and integrity.

Two principles have guided us since 1999, and one belief runs through both. Every machine we sell, and every visit our technicians make, comes back to these.
