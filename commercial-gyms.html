Questions gym owners ask.

How long from order to opening?

It depends on the size of the floor and the machines you choose. Your sales desk gives you a delivery and installation date with the quote.

Do you help with the layout?

Yes. Every commercial order starts with a 3D floor plan, which you can try yourself in the planner.

Can members try the machines first?

Bring your trainers to the Jaipur showroom. Every machine on the site is on the floor.

What happens after installation?

Warranty, servicing and spares are handled by Probodyline. Register your machines on the support page.
